Our Platforms Get in Touch

An integral platform for the region's next leap

Redwood Global Capital is a Singapore-based investment holding company at the heart of Southeast Asia's compute and capital buildout. We pair institutional capital discipline with hands-on operating capability — embedding proven operators from day one and building long-term partnerships grounded in trust and transparency.

Everything we do is deployed through two dedicated platforms: Platform I builds and owns frontier AI & deeptech at the IP level; Platform II develops data-centre infrastructure through an initial phase of up to 300 MW and a long-term target of up to 1 GW. Strategic and public-market pathways are evaluated without assurance of a transaction or timetable.
